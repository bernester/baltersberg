# Freizeit für Alle

The domain of an inclusive summer camp run by a small German association.
Most of the vocabulary is German, and several terms mean something narrower
here than the dictionary suggests. Spellings and how these words are used in
copy are governed by [docs/writing.md](docs/writing.md); this file is only
about what they mean.

## Language

**Freizeit**:
The camp itself — the ten days at the Zeltplatz. Not "free time" or "leisure"
in the general sense. *die Freizeit*, always singular and definite.
_Avoid_: Camp, Ferienlager, holiday

**Freizeit für Alle**:
The name of the camp as an event, and of this site.
_Avoid_: Using it for the association

**Freizeitbande e.V.**:
The registered association that runs the Freizeit. The organisation, where
*Freizeit für Alle* is the thing it organises.
_Avoid_: Verein (in copy), organisation

**VBT**:
Vorbereitungstreffen — the weekend in June where the team plans the Freizeit.
Internal shorthand only (it is also the data key); the site calls this weekend
the *Kick-Off Wochenende*.
_Avoid_: Writing "VBT" anywhere a reader can see it

**Aufbau**:
The days before the Freizeit when the team builds the camp — tents, kitchen,
infrastructure. Paired with Abbau, and a distinct period with its own dates,
not a task.

**Abbau**:
The days after the Freizeit when the camp is taken down again.

**Teamer\*innen**:
The volunteers who run the Freizeit and look after the children. Unpaid, and
addressed as *du*.
_Avoid_: Staff, Betreuer, Personal, helpers

**Buddies**:
The two fixed Teamer\*innen assigned to one tent group — a role within the
team, not a separate group of people.

**Teilnehmende**:
The children and young people who come to the Freizeit.
_Avoid_: Kunden, Gäste, campers

**Preisstufen**:
The three prices for the same place at the Freizeit, chosen by the family
according to household income — middle income, low income, no fixed income.
Self-selected, with no means test. Not discounts, and not tiers of service:
what the child gets is identical.

**Schutzkonzept**:
The association's published safeguarding concept against sexualised violence.
A binding commitment the association holds itself to, not a policy document
filed away.

**Satzung**:
The association's constitution in the legal sense — the statutes a German
e.V. must have and publish.

**Manifest**:
The four principles the association stands for: *bunt, laut, wild, geborgen*.
The brand's foundation, and also a page on the site.

**Anrede**:
Which form of address a page uses — *ihr* for parents, *du* for children, the
team and donors. Fixed per page and per audience, never *Sie*. The rules are
in [docs/writing.md](docs/writing.md).
